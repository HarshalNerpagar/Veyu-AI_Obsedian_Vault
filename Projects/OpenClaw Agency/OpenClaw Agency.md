# OpenClaw Agency

## Overview

A services agency that helps companies and individuals set up, secure, optimize, and maintain OpenClaw AI agents. OpenClaw is an open-source AI agent platform with 175K+ GitHub stars — massive adoption, but most users struggle with installation, security, cost optimization, and enterprise readiness. The agency fills this gap.

**Why now:**
- 5:1 demand-to-supply ratio on freelance platforms (28+ Upwork jobs, 24+ Fiverr gigs, few quality providers)
- 135K+ exposed OpenClaw instances with critical security vulnerabilities — enterprises need help
- No competitor offers a full-stack service (setup + security + optimization + managed support)
- India cost advantage: $20-60/hr vs $100-500/hr Western agencies, enabling 50-75% gross margins
- $7.6B AI agent market growing at 46.3% CAGR to $52.6B by 2030

**Business model:** Agency services (4 tiers) → recurring managed plans → future [[SaaS Products]]

## Key Results

1. **First paying client within 2 weeks** of launch (Fiverr or Upwork)
2. **10 managed clients by month 3** ($1,990/mo recurring)
3. **$10K MRR by month 6** through mix of project work and managed plans

## Notes & Decisions

### Decision: Agency-First Strategy
Start with services to generate cash flow immediately, validate market demand with real clients, then use revenue to fund SaaS product development at month 4-6. This avoids the "build for 6 months then hope someone buys" trap.

### Decision: Security-First Positioning
Lead with security expertise. 135K+ exposed instances and CVE-2026-25253 create urgency. Most competitors ignore security entirely. "We secure your AI agents" is a stronger pitch than "we set up your AI agents."

### Decision: India Cost Advantage
Operating from India enables aggressive pricing that undercuts Western competitors while maintaining 50-75% gross margins. $497 Essential Setup would cost $1,500-3,000 from US agencies.
