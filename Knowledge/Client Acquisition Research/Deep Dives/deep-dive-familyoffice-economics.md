## 3. THE 10% ANALYSIS — WHO SPENDS THE MOST ON TECH

### Profile of the Tech-Forward Top 10%

Based on cross-referencing multiple surveys, the top 10% of tech-spending family offices share these characteristics:

**1. AUM >$500M (necessary but not sufficient)**
- Larger offices have lower cost-per-AUM but higher absolute tech spend
- $1B+ offices spend 35.1 bps vs 41.8 bps for <$250M — but absolute tech $ is 10-20x higher
- Below $500M, tech investment faces the "cost drag" problem — spending $500K on tech when total AUM is $100M is a 0.5% performance drag

**2. Multiple Asset Classes = More Tech Need**
- FOs with direct PE/VC + public markets + real estate + alternatives need consolidated reporting
- 70% of FOs are now actively investing across private markets (Citi 2025)
- Private market data is the #1 technology pain point — "more than 70% of vendors cited private-market ingestion and reconciliation as the most underdeveloped part of their stack" (Simple 2025)
- FOs doing direct deals (63% now, per Dentons) need AI deal sourcing, due diligence automation, portfolio monitoring

**3. Professional (Non-Family) CIOs Drive More Tech Adoption**
- 35% of FOs currently have non-family heads; expected to jump to 49% post-succession (Deloitte)
- Professional CIOs come from institutional backgrounds (GIC, Temasek, hedge funds) and expect institutional-grade tools
- "Offices led by principals with backgrounds in technology or venture investing are moving fastest" (Forbes 2025)
- Professional CIOs are 2-3x more likely to adopt AI tools than family-member-led offices

**4. First-Generation FOs Are MORE Tech-Forward**
- Counter-intuitive finding: 1st gen FOs operate at 36.8 bps vs 44.0 bps for 2nd-7th gen (UBS 2025)
- First-gen founders (often tech/business entrepreneurs) understand technology value
- Multi-gen offices carry legacy systems, institutional inertia, and governance complexity
- BUT: next-gen heirs pushing for tech modernization create a DIFFERENT kind of buyer (succession-driven tech refresh)

**5. FOs Preparing for Generational Transfer**
- 87% haven't transitioned yet; 59% expect to within 10 years (Bank of America 2025)
- Succession planning = technology modernization moment
- "Next-gen leaders expect increased use of AI, automation and digital tools, making tech readiness a strategic priority"
- 69% now have succession plans (up from 53% last year) — tech infrastructure is part of that plan
- $124 TRILLION global wealth transfer underway — families digitizing to prepare

### The Mentality Gap

**Tech-Forward FOs (Top 10%):**
- View technology as competitive advantage
- Measure ROI on tech investments
- Have dedicated IT budget line items
- Pilot AI tools for specific use cases
- Think "build vs. rent" strategically
- Professional CIO + COO driving decisions

**Tech-Laggard FOs (Bottom 50%):**
- View technology as cost center
- "If it ain't broke, don't fix it" mentality
- Rely on Excel, email, and manual processes
- Cybersecurity concerns used as excuse NOT to adopt
- Family principal makes all tech decisions (often poorly)
- "I think most people do not understand or know how to use artificial intelligence, and that most who say they are using it really are not" — FO CIO quote from BlackRock survey

## 4. HYPER-NICHES INSIDE FAMILY OFFICES

### Ranked by Spending Concentration & Veyu Opportunity

#### TIER 1 — HIGHEST SPENDING CONCENTRATION

**1. Portfolio Monitoring & Consolidated Reporting (THE #1 PAIN POINT)**
- 67% identify "improving reporting and data analytics" as top strategic priority (Bank of America 2025)
- 58% plan to increase investment in reporting/analytics tech in next 24 months
- 69% adoption of automated reporting (up from 46%) — but still 31% without
- FOs spend avg 20% of working hours on manual reporting tasks; up to 40% for larger offices
- Reports that "arrive weeks late" are common; some take 3 weeks to compile
- **Market:** $640M+ in FO tech funding in 2024 alone; top platforms (Addepar, Eton Solutions, Asora) processing trillions
- **Veyu angle:** Custom AI reporting dashboards, automated data aggregation from multiple custodians, real-time consolidation across entities

**2. Direct Investment & Deal Sourcing AI**
- 63-70% of FOs now do direct investments (Dentons/Citi 2025)
- Family offices closed deals 40% faster using AI-powered deal flow platforms
- AI deal flow tech stack costs $100K-$300K/year vs $2-5M for PE analyst salaries
- "The information asymmetry that defined private equity's dominance just evaporated"
- 25%+ of private market transactions now involve FOs
- **Veyu angle:** AI-powered deal screening, automated financial modeling from uploaded docs, competitive intelligence, deal pipeline management

**3. Private Market Data Automation**
- 70%+ of vendors say private-market ingestion is "most underdeveloped" part of their stack
- Capital calls, K-1s, fund statements arrive as PDFs — manual extraction is norm
- FOs processing 500+ invoices/month can save $60K+/year on invoice processing alone
- AI reduces processing costs from $12.88 to $2.78 per document
- **Veyu angle:** Document AI for capital call tracking, automated K-1 extraction, fund statement parsing, alternative investment data normalization

#### TIER 2 — HIGH SPENDING, GROWING FAST

**4. Risk Management & Compliance Automation**
- AI deployment in risk/compliance still low — "yet to be integrated across all functions" (Citi 2025)
- 83% identify cybersecurity as biggest operational risk
- Multi-jurisdiction compliance (Singapore + DIFC + home country) creates massive complexity
- 62% of SFOs implement cybersecurity frameworks; 55% use real-time dashboards
- **Veyu angle:** Automated compliance monitoring, multi-jurisdiction regulatory tracking, AML/KYC automation

**5. Tax Optimization & Planning AI**
- 63% outsource tax planning — highest outsourced function (UBS 2025)
- Multi-entity, multi-jurisdiction tax optimization is computationally intensive
- Singapore's tiered 13O/13U requirements create optimization opportunities
- "Automated tax-lot accounting enhancements aligned with 15+ regulatory frameworks"
- **Veyu angle:** AI-driven tax optimization across entities, automated tax reporting, cross-jurisdiction tax planning

**6. Multi-Family Office (MFO) Technology Platforms**
- MFOs projected to grow at 7.96% CAGR through 2031
- MFOs need scalable tech to serve multiple families cost-effectively
- Families below $200M AUM increasingly choosing MFO models
- MFO tech market is consolidating — SEI Archway sold for $120M
- **Veyu angle:** White-label AI tools for MFOs, operational automation platforms, multi-tenant reporting

#### TIER 3 — EMERGING / NICHE

**7. Crypto/Digital Asset Management**
- 33% of FOs globally now hold digital assets (2025, rising in 2026)
- Singapore FOs allocating ~10% to digital assets including tokenized funds and crypto
- MAS Project Guardian driving tokenization adoption
- "A modest pilot of $0.5-2M can generate 35-50% cash-efficiency gains"
- Next-gen heirs view Bitcoin as "digital gold" — generational driver
- **Veyu angle:** Digital asset portfolio integration, tokenized asset tracking, DeFi yield monitoring

**8. Family Governance & Succession Planning Tools**
- $124T wealth transfer underway
- Only ~50% have formal succession plans
- 70% of families lose wealth by 3rd generation
- Tech platforms for governance, communication, wealth blueprints are nascent
- **Veyu angle:** Digital succession planning tools, family governance platforms, next-gen engagement dashboards
