## The Meta-Pattern: Where 10% Control 90%

Every niche we researched follows the same power law. The top slice controls disproportionate spend, but **they're NOT Veyu's target**. The target is the tier just below — firms with money and pain but without internal AI capability.

| Niche | Top 10% Profile | Their AI Spend | Veyu's Sweet Spot | Sweet Spot AI Spend |

| **PE Portfolio Cos** | Mega-funds ($10B+ AUM): Hg, Inflexion, Blackstone | In-house AI teams, proprietary platforms (Hg's Retina, Inflexion's Tearsheet) | UK mid-market: GBP 200M-2B AUM, 10-25 portcos | $200K-$2M/yr tech budget, ZERO AI team |
| **Family Offices** | $1B+ AUM offices (60-100 in Singapore) | $1.5M-$5M+ tech spend, dedicated IT | $250M-$1B AUM, 5-20 staff, 3+ asset classes | $200K-$500K tech spend, mostly manual |
| **InsurTech/Lloyd's** | Ki ($500M capital), Beazley, Brit | Algorithmic syndicates, 27.6% expense ratio | Syndicates #11-25 by GWP (GBP 1-2B) | GBP 3-10M tech, GBP 500K-2M AI |
| **RegTech** | Tier 1 global banks (HSBC, DBS, Standard Chartered) | $50-100M+ compliance tech, 1,000-6,000 compliance staff | Mid-tier banks, fintechs, asset managers, payment firms | $500K-$5M compliance tech, 73% still manual processes |

### The Universal Signal: "Has AI Strategy, No AI Implementation"

This pattern repeats in every niche:
- **PE**: 60%+ of portfolio companies have AI strategy, <15% track AI ROI (Accenture)
- **Family Offices**: 86% invest IN AI companies, only 33% USE AI operationally (Goldman/BlackRock)
- **Lloyd's**: 40% of firms "use AI" but only 14% in underwriting; 65% haven't applied AI to core functions
- **RegTech**: Firms spend millions on compliance staff but manual processes still dominate; 72% admit underinvestment in operational tech

**This is the arbitrage.** Strategy decks exist. Implementation doesn't.
