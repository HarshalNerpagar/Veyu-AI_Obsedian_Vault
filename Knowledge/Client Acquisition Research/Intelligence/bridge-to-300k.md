### BLOCK 1: SCALE BLOCK
**Question:** Can a 3-person team credibly serve $25K/month clients?

#### The Research Says

**Yes, but with constraints.** The research is clear: AI-augmented small teams are now delivering what previously required 10-20 person teams. Jasper AI (a $1.5B valuation company) started as a 3-person team. The key finding from Engineering Team Size research: "The minimum viable team for AI engineering has dropped from 8-12 to 2-3 skilled professionals augmented by AI tools."

#### Team Configuration for Veyu

| Role | Person | Hours/Week on Client Work | Hours/Week on GTM |
|------|--------|--------------------------|-------------------|
| Technical Lead / Architect | Harshal or Rachit | 30 | 10 |
| Project Manager / Client Success | Meghavi | 15 | 25 |
| Second Engineer | Remaining founder | 30 | 10 |

**Maximum concurrent client capacity:** 2 full engagements OR 1 full + 2 lighter retainers.

#### Critical Constraint

At $25K/month per client, serving even 1 client requires ~60 hours/week of engineering time while maintaining GTM activity. This means:

- **Before first client:** No hiring needed. All 3 founders split sales and proof-building.
- **At client 1:** Manageable. One founder leads delivery, one supports, one maintains pipeline.
- **At client 2:** Strain point. Must hire a fractional/contract engineer ($2-4K/month in India).
- **At $25K/mo ARR (2-3 clients):** Need 1 full-time junior engineer. Budget from revenue.

#### How to Build It

1. Define clear role boundaries NOW (who sells, who delivers, who manages).
2. Build SOPs and templates for common deliverables to multiply output.
3. Use AI coding tools (Cursor, Claude Code, GitHub Copilot) as a 4th team member.
4. Plan the first hire at the $15K/month revenue mark, not before.

**Estimated effort:** 1 week to define roles, ongoing to optimize.

### BLOCK 2: PRESENTATION BLOCK
**Question:** How must Veyu present itself to command $15-25K/month?

#### The Research Says

The research on B2B credibility is emphatic: **"60% to 90% of the B2B buyer journey occurs before vendor awareness."** Buyers review 15+ content pieces before engaging. At the $25K/month level, the buyer is not evaluating "can they code?" -- they are evaluating "can I trust them with a critical business outcome?"

#### What a $25K/month Agency Looks Like vs. a $2K Freelancer

| Element | $2K Freelancer | $25K/month Agency |
|---------|---------------|-------------------|
| Website | Template, generic services | Custom, outcome-focused, industry-specific |

Based on analysis of premium AI agency websites (Invisible Technologies, NineTwoThree, InData Labs):

1. **Homepage:** Problem-focused headline (NOT "We do AI"). Example: "We help [niche] companies turn [specific pain] into [specific outcome] using AI."
2. **How We Work page:** 3-phase engagement model (Discovery > Build > Optimize). Demonstrates process maturity.
3. **Case Studies page:** Even with zero clients, populate with spec work, demos, and technical deep-dives.
4. **Team page:** Full bios with relevant credentials. Photo + LinkedIn link for each founder.
5. **Contact/Book a Call:** Calendly embed. No friction.

### BLOCK 3: PRICING PSYCHOLOGY BLOCK
**Question:** How to anchor at $15-25K/month from day one?

#### The Research Says

The pricing data from 2026 is clear on market rates:

| Engagement Type | Freelance Rate | Agency Rate |
|-----------------|---------------|-------------|
| Monthly retainer | $3K-$10K | $10K-$30K+ |
| Strategy project | $10K-$30K | $30K-$80K |

India-based agencies typically charge $30-$80/hour vs. US-based at $150-$350/hour. But premium Indian agencies (like GeekyAnts, Uniphore) successfully charge $51-$100/hour by positioning on outcomes, not geography.

#### The Pricing Structure That Works

**Never sell hours. Never sell "AI development." Always sell outcomes.**

**Recommended 3-Tier Model:**

| Tier | Name | Monthly Price | What Client Gets |
|------|------|--------------|------------------|
| 1 | AI Audit + Pilot | $5,000-$8,000 (one-time) | 2-week assessment, prototype, ROI roadmap |
| 2 | Growth | $12,000-$18,000/mo | 1-2 AI systems, optimization, monthly strategy |
| 3 | Enterprise | $20,000-$30,000/mo | Dedicated team, multiple systems, priority support |

**The Gateway Drug: AI Readiness Audit ($5K)**
Based on Digital Applied's "AI Audit Gateway" model -- a $5K diagnostic engagement that:
- Requires minimal client commitment
- Demonstrates Veyu expertise
- Produces a roadmap that naturally leads to implementation
- Recovers CAC within 30 days (per Veyu payback period obsession)

**Anchor high, negotiate scope, never negotiate price.**

The Value Equation framing:
- "Our $15K/month retainer typically saves clients $50-100K/year in [specific area]" (3-7x ROI)
- "The $5K audit pays for itself in the first recommendation we implement"

#### Pricing from India -- The Perception Problem and Solution

Research confirms that "India-based" can trigger commodity perceptions. The counter:
1. **Never lead with location.** Lead with expertise and outcomes.
2. **Price based on client market, not yours.** A Singapore-based client willingness to pay is based on Singapore AI consulting rates ($150-300/hour), not Indian rates.
3. **Use the cost arbitrage silently.** Your margins are extraordinary at $15K/month because your costs are Indian. Do not advertise this.
4. **Frame the team as "global" not "Indian."** "Our team operates across IST, SGT, and GMT time zones" is better than "We are based in India."

**Estimated effort:** 1 week to build pricing deck and ROI models.

### BLOCK 4: PROOF BLOCK
**Question:** Zero case studies is the biggest gap. How to create proof without clients?

#### The Research Says

The Reddit/LinkedIn research consensus: "Build two micro case studies, even if they are demo builds." The market expects proof, but the bar for early-stage proof is lower than most founders assume.

#### 7 Ways to Create Proof Without Clients

**Tier 1: High Impact (do these first)**

1. **Niche-Specific Demo Builds (2-3 weeks each)**
   Build a working prototype that solves a real problem in each target niche:
   - **RegTech Singapore:** Demo AML transaction monitoring system using synthetic data
   - **PE Portfolio Companies:** Build a portfolio company performance dashboard with AI-generated insights
   - **Mining/Australia:** Predictive maintenance alert system using public sensor data

   Each demo should include: live URL, 2-minute Loom walkthrough, quantified "what this would save" analysis.

2. **Technical Deep-Dive Articles (1 week each)**
   Publish 3-5 LinkedIn articles showing domain expertise:
   - "How AI is Reducing False Positive Rates in RegTech AML Monitoring by 85%"
   - "The 3 AI Use Cases PE Firms Should Deploy Across Portfolio Companies in 2026"
   - "Predictive Maintenance in Mining: What the Data Actually Shows"

3. **Open-Source Contributions**
   Contribute to relevant open-source projects (LangChain, LlamaIndex, compliance tools). Creates verifiable proof of technical ability.

**Tier 2: Supporting Proof**

4. **"Results We Would Have Delivered" Mock Case Studies**
   Take a public company known problem, analyze it, and write up: "If [Company] had deployed [specific AI solution], they could have achieved [X outcome]. Here is exactly how." Anonymize as needed.

5. **Conference Talks and Webinars**
   Apply to speak at niche-specific conferences (Singapore FinTech Festival, UK PE conferences, Australian Mining events). Even rejected proposals build a track record of expertise.

6. **Certifications and Partnerships**
   AWS AI/ML certifications, Google Cloud AI certifications, Microsoft AI certifications. Quick trust signal. Many are free or low-cost.

7. **Loom Video Walkthroughs**
   Record 90-second videos showing how you would solve specific problems. Attach to outreach emails. According to research, Loom-enhanced proposals get significantly higher response rates.

**Estimated effort:** 3-4 weeks for Tier 1 items. Ongoing for Tier 2.

### BLOCK 7: RISK REVERSAL BLOCK
**Question:** What guarantees or structures reduce buyer risk?

#### The Research Says

The guarantee template research from RiverEditor (2026) provides hard data:
- **Paid pilot model:** +67% initial engagement rate, 84% pilot-to-contract conversion
- **Performance guarantee:** +89% close rate on qualified leads
- **Satisfaction guarantee:** +23% proposal acceptance rate
- **Strong guarantees DECREASE refund rates by 8-15%** -- they attract confident buyers

#### Veyu Risk Reversal Framework

**The Pilot-First Model (Recommended for First 5 Clients)**

```
ENGAGEMENT STRUCTURE:

Phase 0: FREE Discovery Call (30 min)
  --> Qualify. Understand their problem. Show domain knowledge.

Phase 1: Paid AI Audit ($5,000-$8,000, 2 weeks)
  --> Deliverable: Written report with ROI projections + working prototype
  --> GUARANTEE: If the audit does not identify at least 3 AI opportunities
     with >3x ROI potential, full refund.
  --> This fee is credited toward Phase 2 if they proceed.

Phase 2: Pilot Implementation ($15,000-$25,000, 4-6 weeks)
  --> Build 1 AI system from the audit recommendations
  --> GUARANTEE: If the system does not achieve [specific metric]
     within 30 days of deployment, Veyu continues working at no
     additional cost until it does.
  --> 50% upfront, 50% on delivery.

Phase 3: Ongoing Retainer ($12,000-$20,000/month)
  --> Optimization + additional systems
  --> Month-to-month contract (no lock-in)
  --> 30-day cancellation notice
```

**Why This Works:**
- Client risk at entry: $5,000 (digestible even for cautious buyers)
- Veyu recovers CAC in Phase 1 (within 30 days per the payback period rule)
- 84% of pilots convert to full engagements (industry data)
- Month-to-month retainer shows confidence in delivery quality
- No price discounting -- risk reversal happens through structure, not price cuts

#### Niche-Specific Risk Reversal Adjustments

| Niche | Best Risk Reversal | Why |
|-------|-------------------|-----|
| PE Firms | Speed guarantee ("prototype in 2 weeks or free") | PE values speed |
| Family Offices | Discretion + exclusivity ("we serve max 3 family offices") | They value privacy |
| RegTech | Compliance guarantee ("meets [regulation] or we fix free") | Regulatory risk is #1 fear |
| Mining/Super Funds | Performance metric guarantee ("reduce [X] by [Y]%") | Want measurable impact |

**Estimated effort:** 1 week to build proposal templates with risk reversal language.

## 4. The "First Client" Profile -- Deep Analysis

### Most Accessible First Client by Niche (Ranked by Speed to Close)

**#1 MOST ACCESSIBLE: PE Portfolio Company (UK)**
- **Why fastest:** PE firms actively push portfolio companies to adopt AI. The PE firm makes the introduction. Decision cycles are short because the PE firm is applying pressure.
- **Entry point:** Reach the PE firm operating partner or value creation team.
- **Deal size:** $8-15K pilot, potentially $15-25K/month retainer across multiple portfolio companies.
- **Close timeline:** 3-6 weeks if introduced by the PE firm.

**#2: Funded Startup CTO (Singapore)**
- **Why accessible:** Singapore startup ecosystem is tight-knit. Indian founders in Singapore are well-represented.
- **Entry point:** LinkedIn + Indian diaspora network + startup communities.
- **Deal size:** $5-10K pilot, $10-15K/month retainer.
- **Close timeline:** 3-5 weeks.

**#3: RegTech Company (Singapore)**
- **Why accessible:** Genuine, urgent AI need driven by regulatory pressure.
- **Entry point:** Singapore FinTech Association events, LinkedIn targeting.
- **Deal size:** $8-15K pilot, $15-25K/month retainer.
- **Close timeline:** 4-8 weeks (compliance review adds time).

**#4: Mining Company (Australia)**
- **Why less accessible initially:** Longer sales cycles, more procurement layers.

## 6. Risk Factors and Mitigation

### RISK 1: 55-Day Runway is Dangerously Short
**Severity:** CRITICAL
**Probability:** High (enterprise sales average 90 days)

**Mitigation:**
- Target SMB/startup clients, NOT enterprise (shorter sales cycle)
- Use the paid audit model ($5K) to generate revenue FAST
- If Day 30 passes with no revenue, take 1-2 freelance/Upwork projects ($3-5K each) to extend runway
- Consider a bridge: one founder takes a part-time consulting gig to fund the other two

### RISK 2: Zero Proof Kills Credibility
**Severity:** HIGH
**Probability:** Medium (mitigated by demo builds)

**Mitigation:**
- Demo projects are the #1 priority in Week 1-2
- Each demo must solve a REAL problem, not be a toy
- Loom walkthroughs make demos accessible to non-technical buyers
- Technical LinkedIn posts serve as proof of depth

### RISK 3: Pricing Too High for First Client
**Severity:** MEDIUM
**Probability:** Medium

**Mitigation:**
- The $5K audit is the entry point -- manageable for most companies
- NEVER lower price. Reduce scope instead.
- "For our first 3 clients, we are offering the AI Audit at $5K with a full credit toward implementation"

### RISK 4: "India-Based" Perception
**Severity:** MEDIUM
**Probability:** Medium (depends on niche)

**Mitigation:**
- Lead with expertise, never geography
- Video calls establish personal connection
- Proposal quality must be EXCEPTIONAL
- Consider having one founder attend a Singapore or London event in person

### RISK 5: Spreading Too Thin Across 3 Geographies and 6 Niches
**Severity:** HIGH
**Probability:** High

**Mitigation:**
- **Pick ONE niche for the first 30 days.** Recommendation: PE Portfolio Companies (UK) OR RegTech (Singapore).
- Do not attempt all 3 geographies simultaneously.
- All 3 founders align on the same niche for outreach coordination.
- Expand only after first client is signed.

### RISK 6: Team Burnout
**Severity:** HIGH
**Probability:** High

**Mitigation:**
- Clear role delineation (Meghavi: strategy + sales; Harshal: outreach + calls; Rachit: content + LinkedIn)
- No one person works more than 60 hours/week
- Weekly 30-minute team sync
- Celebrate small wins

### RISK 7: Sales Cycle Exceeds Runway
**Severity:** CRITICAL
**Probability:** Medium

**Mitigation:**
- The audit model ($5K, 2-week engagement) shortens the sales cycle to 2-4 weeks
- Multiple prospects in pipeline simultaneously (aim for 5+ active conversations by Day 21)
- "Break glass" plan: if no revenue by Day 40, each founder does freelance work to extend runway by 30 days

```
Scenario A: The Steady Build
  Month 1-2: 1 client at $12K/month            = $12K/mo
  Month 3-4: Add 1 client at $15K/month         = $27K/mo  (ACHIEVED $25K+)
  Month 5-8: Maintain + optimize
  Month 9-12: Add 1 more at $15K/month          = $42K/mo  ($500K ARR run rate)

Scenario B: The PE Multiplier (Dream Scenario)
  Month 1: Land PE relationship, pilot 1 portfolio co at $8K
  Month 2: Convert to $15K/month retainer
  Month 3: PE rolls out to 2 more portfolio companies ($30K)
  Month 3: Total = $45K/month ($540K ARR run rate)

Scenario C: The Blended Model
  Month 1-2: 2 audits at $5K each = $10K one-time
  Month 2-3: Convert 1 audit to $12K/month retainer
  Month 3-5: 3 more audits + 1 conversion to $15K/month

```
