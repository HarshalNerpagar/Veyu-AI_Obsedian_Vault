## Scoring Methodology

All niches scored across 8 dimensions (weighted /1000), then normalized to /100:

| Dimension | Weight | Why |
|-----------|--------|-----|
| Revenue Proximity | 20% | Closer to client revenue = higher perceived value = faster close |
| Client Budget Capacity | 20% | Can they afford $5K-$25K/month? |
| Pain Acuteness | 15% | Severity of pain without AI |
| Market Size | 10% | Enough companies to sustain pipeline? |
| Growth Trajectory | 10% | Growing market = expanding demand |
| Competition Density | 10% | Fewer competitors = easier win |
| Veyu Fit | 10% | Does Veyu's AI engineering capability match? |
| Accessibility | 5% | Can a 3-person India-based agency reach decision makers? |

**Must-Pass Gates** (any FAIL = niche rejected):
1. Has Money ($5K+/month threshold)
2. Has Real Pain (measurable cost of status quo)
3. Growing Market (positive trajectory)

## The PE + Family Office Convergence (Key Strategic Insight)

The most powerful finding from 3,116 lines of research:

**PE firms and family offices are converging.** Family offices are the capital providers behind PE funds. The same AI capabilities that help PE portfolio companies (implementation, operational AI) also help family offices (deal sourcing, portfolio monitoring, reporting).

Veyu can position at the **intersection**:
- Build AI for PE portfolio companies (implementation work, $180K-$900K/yr per fund)
- Simultaneously serve the family offices that FUND those PE firms (analytics, deal sourcing, monitoring, $120K-$300K/yr)
- One relationship ecosystem, two revenue streams
- Dubai and Singapore are where both markets are hottest simultaneously

**No competitor is targeting both with the same team and methodology.**
