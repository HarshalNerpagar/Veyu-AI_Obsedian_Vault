### Superannuation — The Mega Opportunity

Australia's superannuation system manages **$4.5 trillion** (December 2025), with $3.2 trillion in APRA-regulated funds. This is projected to become the **world's second-largest retirement system by 2031**.

Key dynamics:
- **Rapid consolidation:** Number of funds shrinking dramatically — heading toward 20-30 mega funds
- **933 APRA-regulated funds** currently, but consolidating fast
- **Payday Super** (effective July 2026) — requires real-time super payments, massive tech overhaul
- **CPS 230 compliance** — new APRA operational resilience standard requiring tech uplift
- AI use cases: data reconciliation, member behavior prediction, retirement planning, compliance automation, investment optimization
- Technology providers: Bravura Solutions, SS&C Technologies, Class Super, BGL (admin platforms)

### Regulatory Drivers

- APRA intensifying cyber resilience supervision for super funds (June 2025 letter on critical authentication controls)
- ASIC focus on AI use, scam prevention, greenwashing
- New AML/CTF regulations (Tranche 2) effective 2026 — requiring technology solutions
- No specific AI regulation yet — opportunity window before rules constrain

### Pain Points

1. **Legacy system migration** — super funds running on decades-old platforms
2. **Payday Super compliance** — massive technology overhaul needed by July 2026
3. **Member engagement at scale** — 3.5M+ members per mega fund
4. **Investment data complexity** — private markets, alternative assets, real-time IBOR/PBOR
5. **Regulatory compliance burden** — CPS 230, ASIC best financial interest duty, AML/CTF
6. **Only 18% of professionals track AI ROI** — need help measuring and demonstrating value
