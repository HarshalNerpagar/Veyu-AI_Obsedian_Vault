## 4-Company Test Calculations

The 4-Company Test: If we can win just 4 clients at $25K/month each, that equals $1.2M/year.

### 1. PE Portfolio Companies
- **4 clients at $25K/mo = $1.2M/year**
- Probability of winning 4: **HIGH**. There are 100s of mid-market PE firms across target markets. 74% of portcos need external AI partners. The gap in mid-market implementation (not strategy) is real.
- Path to 4: Target 2 UK mid-market PE firms + 1 Singapore + 1 UAE. Offer "AI Value Creation Playbook" service at $20-30K/month per portfolio company.
- With a single PE fund deploying across 5-10 portfolio companies, one PE relationship could be $100K-$300K/month.

### 2. Family Offices
- **4 clients at $25K/mo = $1.2M/year**
- Probability of winning 4: **HIGH**. 1,289 in DIFC alone. 2,000+ in Singapore. 86% using AI but only at "early stage." Almost no specialized AI agencies targeting them.
- Path to 4: Target 2 Dubai FOs + 2 Singapore FOs. Offer AI-powered deal sourcing + portfolio monitoring + consolidated reporting.
- FOs with $500M+ AUM can easily justify $25K/month for AI that saves 20+ hours/week of analyst time.

### 3. Longevity Clinics
- **4 clients at $25K/mo = $1.2M/year**
- Probability of winning 4: **LOW-MODERATE**. Only ~320 clinics globally. Most are small businesses. Top chains (Fountain Life, Clinique La Prairie) build in-house.
- Path to 4: Target 2 Dubai clinics + 1 Singapore + 1 London. But revenue per client may be lower ($10-15K/month more realistic).
- At $15K/month x 4 = $720K/year. Decent but not $1.2M.

### 4. Luxury Brands
- **4 clients at $25K/mo = $1.2M/year**
