### The Anti-Fragile Three (GET STRONGER during crises)

1. **RegTech/Compliance AI** -- Every crisis since 2008 triggered MORE regulation, MORE compliance costs, MORE financial crime. Compliance spending rose 60%+ post-2008. Iran war sanctions creating new compliance complexity globally. MAS increasing scrutiny. This niche GROWS under every stress scenario.

2. **PE Portfolio Companies** -- When exits freeze, hold periods extend 3-5 years. PE firms pivot from financial engineering to operational value creation. AI becomes THE value creation lever. Best PE vintages in history were deployed during recessions. BCG: 74% of portcos need external AI partners.

3. **InsurTech/Lloyd's** -- War, catastrophe, and conflict CREATE insurance demand. Lloyd's posted GBP 10.6B profit in 2025. War risk insurance is booming. AI risk analytics become critical during crisis periods.

### The Evergreen Two (STABLE regardless of conditions)

4. **Family Offices** -- Multi-generational capital. Don't panic-sell. Maintain operational spending. Geography shifts (UAE→SG) but total demand stays flat. 61% cite geopolitical conflict as #1 risk -- drives demand for risk management AI.

5. **Super Fund Admin AI** -- Payday Super deadline (July 2026) is regulatory, not market-dependent. $4.5T sector doesn't disappear in a recession. Compliance requirements increase under stress.

### The Conditional Two (VIABLE with reframing)

6. **Mining Predictive Maintenance** -- Commodity prices may drop, but maintenance costs don't. 50% of opex is maintenance. Position as cost REDUCTION during downturn, not growth. ROI argument gets STRONGER: "save $2M-$10M/yr on a $180K-$300K investment."

7. **UAE DIFC Fintech** -- PAUSED due to active conflict. KYC/AML compliance demand remains (sanctions screening actually increases). Will resume post-ceasefire. Compliance angle survives even during war.

### The Casualty

8. **UAE PropTech** -- Dubai property crashed 40-50% in 2008. Current war creates direct physical threats. Developer share prices down 13-17% in first week. DEPRIORITIZE -- move to backlog until UAE stabilizes AND property market recovers.
