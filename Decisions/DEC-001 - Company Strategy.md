# DEC-001 — Company Strategy: Agency-First, Product Later

## Decision
Veyu AI will operate as an AI/tech services agency to generate cash flow, build network, and establish market presence. When we have stable revenue, a validated problem, and capital, we'll transition to building a product (SaaS or otherwise).

## Context
All three founders want to build a product company, but:
- Finding real product-market fit takes time and research
- We need cash flow to sustain ourselves and fund R&D
- We need market exposure to discover real problems worth solving
- We have the skills to deliver services immediately

## Options Considered
1. **Jump straight to product** — High risk, no revenue, long runway needed
2. **Agency-first, product later** — Revenue from day one, builds toward product
3. **Part-time freelancing + part-time product** — Split focus, slow on both

## Why This Choice
Option 2 gives us everything: cash flow, network, market insights, and a natural pipeline to product. Agency work IS market research — every client interaction reveals problems worth solving at scale.

## Who Was Involved
All three co-founders. Unanimous.

## Impact
- Immediate focus: client acquisition, service delivery, SOPs
- Product research happens passively through client work
- Revenue targets drive short-term goals

## Review Date
2026-06-14 (3 months — reassess based on revenue and product ideas)
