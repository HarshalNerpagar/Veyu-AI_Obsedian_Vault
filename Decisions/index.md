# Decision Log

Important decisions with full context. Prevents "who decided this?" and "why did we do it that way?"

## Template
Use `Templates/Decision` for new entries. Number sequentially: DEC-001, DEC-002, etc.

## Decisions
- [[DEC-001 - Company Strategy]] — Agency-first, product later

## Conventions
- Log ANY decision that affects more than one person
- Include who was involved, what was decided, and WHY
- Review past decisions in retrospectives — some may need revisiting
