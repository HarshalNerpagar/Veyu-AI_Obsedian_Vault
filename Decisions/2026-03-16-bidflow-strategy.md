# DEC-002 — BidFlow as Veyu AI's Primary Product Focus

> [!ai-generated]
> Generated by Claude on 2026-03-16

## Decision

BidFlow becomes Veyu AI's primary product focus. We build an AI-powered Contractor Operating System for Indian government contractors, starting with WhatsApp-native tender consulting and expanding into billing optimization, cost estimation, working capital facilitation, procurement aggregation, and a full Contractor OS.

Agency work continues for cash flow, but BidFlow is the product bet.

## Context

Since founding Veyu AI, the thesis has been: agency-first for cash flow, product when we find the right problem. Through extensive research in March 2026, we identified a massive gap in the Indian government contracting space.

Research conducted across:
- **Claude Code** — 7 parallel research agents (licensing, execution, tech, market, vertical integration, construction dynamics, fit analysis)
- **Claude.ai** — Revenue model refinement, commission enforcement
- **Google Gemini** — Market sizing, government policy, 3.85% mandated commission
- **ChatGPT/Grok** — Cross-validation
- **Manus.ai** — Competitive landscape, Powerplay disconnect analysis

All 5 platforms independently converged: viable business, clear path, right timing, 18-24 month competitive window.

## Options Considered

1. **Pure Tender Consulting** — WhatsApp-based tender prep service. Commission + per-bid fees.
   - Pros: Simple to start, low capital, immediate revenue
   - Cons: Low ceiling (~Rs 1.2L/contractor/year), no moat, easily copied

2. **Vertical Integration (Become a Contractor)** — Actually bid on and execute government contracts.
   - Pros: Higher absolute revenue per project
   - Cons: 3-8% margins, Rs 50L+ capital needed, 6/8 fatal capability gaps, Katerra lost $2.1B trying this. See [[Why Not Vertical Integration]].

3. **Contractor Operating System with 6 Revenue Streams** — Start with tenders as acquisition, expand to billing, estimation, fintech, procurement, and SaaS.
   - Pros: Rs 5-14L/year per contractor, 70%+ margins, data flywheel, OfBusiness-proven model
   - Cons: Longer time to full-stack, requires sequential buildout over 24 months

## Why This Choice

Option 3 because:

1. **Highest ceiling.** Rs 5-14L/year per contractor vs Rs 1.2L for pure consulting. 10,000 contractors = Rs 140Cr ARR.
2. **Plays to our strengths.** AI-native team (Meghavi's backend/AI, Rachit's frontend, Harshal's business). We build software, not construction.
3. **Proven model.** OfBusiness ran this exact playbook in SME procurement → Rs 19,000Cr revenue, $5B valuation. We run it in government contracting.
4. **Massive TAM.** Rs 50-70 lakh crore government procurement. Even 0.01% = Rs 500Cr.
5. **Data moat.** After 5,000 contractors, bid data + cost data + billing patterns = competitive moat that's nearly unbreachable.
6. **Timing.** 18-24 month window before well-funded competition likely enters. No one does Bid + Bill + OS + Fintech together today.
